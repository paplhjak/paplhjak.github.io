Selective classification allows a model to abstain from uncertain predictions. SCOD extends this setting to test inputs that may be out of distribution. The paper characterizes the optimal strategy, studies its learnability, and introduces POSCOD, a plugin estimator learned from in-distribution data and an unlabeled mixture.
