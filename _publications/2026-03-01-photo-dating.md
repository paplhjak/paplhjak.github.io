We introduce a face-based approach to estimating the capture year of photographs. The method combines modern face recognition and age estimation with career-based temporal priors, aggregating evidence across all identifiable people in an image. The work also releases CSFD-1.6M, a dataset with more than 1.6 million annotated faces.
